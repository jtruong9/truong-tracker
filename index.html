How to use

A tracker for work and personal items, kept in your own Google Drive folder.

The idea

There are two sections, Work and Personal. Each holds categories (for example Projects or Home), each category holds items (one project, one chore), and each item can have a checklist of steps.

Adding things

  • Category: open Work or Personal and click Add category.
  • Item, fast: type in the Add an item box at the bottom of a category and press Enter.
  • Item, with details: click Add item at the top, or the + on a category.
  • Checklist step: open an item's row with its arrow and type in Add a step, or edit the item.

Each item

  • Status: To do, In progress, Blocked or Done. Change it from the drop-down on the row.
  • Priority (High, Medium, Low) and a due date. Late items turn red.
  • Notes and links (a Jira ticket, a GitLab MR, a Confluence page). Links open in a new tab.
  • Click an item's title to edit everything, move it to another category, or delete it.

Today

One list of what needs you: items that are overdue, due today, due in the next 7 days, blocked or in progress, across both sections or just one.

Finding things

The search box at the top filters the page you are on by title, notes, checklist steps and link names. On Work and Personal you can show open items, all items or only done ones, and filter by priority.

Your data

  • Everything is saved in the folder you chose, as tracker-<computer>.json files. Each computer writes only its own file, so Google Drive never makes conflict copies; every computer reads all of them.
  • Changes from your other computer show up when you come back to this window, or with the reload button at the top.
  • Settings › Back up downloads one file with everything. Load from a file brings a backup back.
  • ⚠ Work items live in your personal Google Drive. Keep CUI, ITAR / EAR and proprietary details out of titles, notes and checklists: names and status only.

On your iPhone

  • Open the site in Safari and tap Sign in with Google. Use the Google account whose Drive holds Truong Tracker Data; the phone then reads and saves that same folder (it adds its own tracker-<phone>.json).
  • Tap Share (the square with the arrow), then Add to Home Screen. Open it from the home screen from then on, like an app.
  • The phone keeps a copy of your list, so it opens and edits with no signal. The line at the bottom of the menu says when it last reached Drive; an edit made offline is saved to Drive the next time the phone has signal and the app is open.
  • About once an hour Google's sign-in runs out. The app goes back to Google for a moment and returns on its own; if Google wants your password, sign in again.
  • Changes from your computers appear when you open the app, switch back to it, or pull the reload button.

Install and offline (computers)

Use Install at the top to add it as an app. It opens without internet; Google Drive syncs your changes when you are back online.

Settings

Data folder, backups and loading items from a file.

Data folder

Back up

Downloads one file with every category and item. Keep it somewhere safe; it is not needed day to day.

Load from a file

Brings in a backup, or a starter file of categories and items. Anything in the file that is already here (the same item) is replaced by the file's copy; everything else is added. Nothing is deleted.
